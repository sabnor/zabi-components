## Overlap with the 8.1.x gap fixes (read before starting)

- **Z-036 / Z-037 (generator).** `neutralChroma` and per-mode `overrides`
  (`light`, `dark`, `both`) now exist. Any new 9.0 surface, wash or material
  token a consumer should be able to retint must be settable through that
  mechanism and must restate its dark value in the dark rules. The ink roles
  follow the ramp only with `neutralChroma`; 9.0's material tokens must do the
  same (derive from the ramp, not literal black or white).
- **Z-038 (field edge).** `--color-input-border` is base-500 and guarded at 3:1
  on the field fill, the page and the card. Packages 1 and 2 move the page and
  card: rerun that guard; the overlay surface in dark is not in it.
- **Z-039 (Progress).** Progress reads `--color-progress-fill` and
  `--color-progress-track`. Status-colour retuning (package 3) and gradients
  (package 5) must not bypass them.
- The generator's pair list is shared with the library guard
  (`scripts/contrast-pairs.js`); each new guarded pair changes the theme
  snapshot and the byte hashes in `tests/create-theme.test.js`.
